FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Update stuck at the same percent? Stop the update services, rename the download cache, start them again.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Stop-Service -Name wuauserv, bits -Force
Rename-Item "$env:SystemRoot\SoftwareDistribution" `
  -NewName SoftwareDistribution.old
Start-Service -Name wuauserv, bits

Heads up: Update history resets. Delete the .old folder later once updates work. Why it works: a corrupt download cache blocks every retry. Windows rebuilds a fresh one. Ticket closed. (Resolution: update unstuck.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Look at what's frozen before you kill anything. Step one lists it. Step two closes it.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
# Step 1: list the frozen ones
Get-Process | Where-Object { -not $_.Responding }
# Step 2: close them
Get-Process | Where-Object { -not $_.Responding } | Stop-Process -Force

Heads up: Unsaved work in those apps is gone. Check the list first. Why it works: Windows flags apps that stop answering, so you can target only those. Ticket closed. (Resolution: killed.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Printer says "printing" for three hours? Don't reinstall it. Restart the spooler and clear the queue.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Stop-Service -Name Spooler -Force
Remove-Item "$env:SystemRoot\System32\spool\PRINTERS\*" -Force
Start-Service -Name Spooler

Heads up: Clears every queued print job. Save your work first. Why it works: jobs wait in a folder. One bad file blocks the rest. Ticket closed. (Resolution: spooler.)

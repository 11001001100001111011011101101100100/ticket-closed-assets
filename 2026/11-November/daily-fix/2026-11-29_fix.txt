FACEBOOK CAPTION:
"I restart it every night." Let's check. Shut down is not restart when Fast Startup is on.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
$boot = (Get-CimInstance Win32_OperatingSystem).LastBootUpTime
(Get-Date) - $boot

Heads up: Use Restart, not Shut down, for a full reboot. Why it works: Fast Startup saves the kernel session, so Shut down skips a true boot. Ticket closed. (Resolution: restart (the real one).)

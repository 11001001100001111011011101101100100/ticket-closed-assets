FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Before you touch the registry, drivers or updates, make a restore point. It takes ten seconds and saves the weekend.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Checkpoint-Computer -Description "Before the fix" `
  -RestorePointType MODIFY_SETTINGS

Heads up: Windows allows one restore point per 24 hours by default. Why it works: you get a clean way back if the fix makes things worse. Ticket closed. (Resolution: restore point.)

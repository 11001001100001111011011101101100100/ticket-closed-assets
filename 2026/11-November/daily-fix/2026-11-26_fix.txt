FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Two commands tell you which one. Name lookup first, then a connection test. It's DNS more often than anyone admits.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Resolve-DnsName microsoft.com
Test-NetConnection microsoft.com -Port 443

Heads up: Lookup fails? DNS. Lookup works but the test fails? Check the network or firewall. Why it works: the first command tests names, the second tests the path. Ticket closed. (Resolution: it was DNS.)

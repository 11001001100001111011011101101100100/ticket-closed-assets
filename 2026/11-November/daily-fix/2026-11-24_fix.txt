FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Random crashes, broken apps, odd errors? Repair the Windows image first, then scan the system files.

Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run:
DISM /Online /Cleanup-Image /RestoreHealth
sfc /scannow

Heads up: Let both finish. Closing the window halfway helps nobody. Why it works: DISM repairs the source files, then sfc uses them to fix the rest. Ticket closed. (Resolution: sfc and DISM.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Find out what is keeping it awake, and what woke it last.

Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run:
powercfg /requests
powercfg /lastwake

Heads up: Empty lists mean nothing is blocking sleep right now. Why it works: it names the app, driver or device holding Windows awake. Ticket closed. (Resolution: sleep restored.)

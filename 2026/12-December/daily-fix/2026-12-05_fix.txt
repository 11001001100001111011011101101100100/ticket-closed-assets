FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Start Outlook without add-ins. Press Win+R and type one of these.

Press Win+R and run:
outlook.exe /safe
# Still odd? Reset the navigation pane
outlook.exe /resetnavpane

Heads up: /resetnavpane resets your folder pane layout and favorites. Why it works: a bad add-in is a common reason Outlook hangs on startup. Ticket closed. (Resolution: safe mode.)

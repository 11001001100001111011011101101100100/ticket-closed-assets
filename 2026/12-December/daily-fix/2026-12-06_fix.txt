FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Search finds nothing? Restart the search service before you rebuild anything.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Restart-Service -Name WSearch
Get-Service -Name WSearch

Heads up: Still empty after that? Rebuild the index in Indexing Options. Why it works: the service can hang, and a restart is quicker than a rebuild. Ticket closed. (Resolution: reindexed.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Before you reinstall drivers, restart the audio service.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Restart-Service -Name Audiosrv -Force

Heads up: Audio may cut out for a second while it restarts. Why it works: the audio service can stall after sleep, a dock change or an update. Ticket closed. (Resolution: sound back.)

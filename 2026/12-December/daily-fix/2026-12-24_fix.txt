FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Everything that launches at sign-in slows you down. List it, then switch off what you don't need.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-CimInstance Win32_StartupCommand |
  Select-Object Name, Command, Location

Heads up: This only lists them. Turn items off in Task Manager under Startup apps. Why it works: every item here starts at sign-in and competes for resources. Ticket closed. (Resolution: startup trimmed.)

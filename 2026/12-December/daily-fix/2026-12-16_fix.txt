FACEBOOK CAPTION:
"The trust relationship failed." Test the secure channel first. Repair it if it's broken.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Test-ComputerSecureChannel -Verbose
Test-ComputerSecureChannel -Repair -Credential (Get-Credential)

Heads up: Needs a network path to a domain controller and rights to reset the account. Why it works: the PC's password with the domain drifted, and this resets it. Ticket closed. (Resolution: trust restored.)

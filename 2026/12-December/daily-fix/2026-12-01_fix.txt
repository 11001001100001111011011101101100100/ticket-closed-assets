FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Drop the dead mapping and map it again. Swap in your own drive letter and path.

Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run:
net use Z: /delete
net use Z: \\fileserver\share /persistent:yes

Heads up: Z: and \\fileserver\share are examples. Use your own. Why it works: a stale session to the server keeps the old mapping broken. Ticket closed. (Resolution: drive remapped.)

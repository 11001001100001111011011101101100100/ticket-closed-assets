FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Run the online scan first. It checks the disk without taking the PC down.

Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run:
chkdsk C: /scan
# Errors found? chkdsk C: /f (needs a reboot)

Heads up: Back up first if the disk is making noises or SMART is failing. Why it works: most file system problems show up in a scan before they become a crash. Ticket closed. (Resolution: disk checked.)

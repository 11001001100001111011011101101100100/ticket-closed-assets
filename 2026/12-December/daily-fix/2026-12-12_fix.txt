FACEBOOK CAPTION:
When nothing else works, reset the Winsock catalog and the IP stack, then reboot.

Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run:
netsh winsock reset
netsh int ip reset

Heads up: Custom network settings, including static IPs, may be reset. Note them first. Why it works: VPN clients and security tools can corrupt the network stack. Ticket closed. (Resolution: stack reset.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Before you blame the hardware, check what's actually holding the memory.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-Process | Sort-Object WorkingSet64 -Descending |
  Select-Object -First 10 Name,
  @{n='MB'; e={[int]($_.WorkingSet64/1MB)}}

Heads up: Browsers split into many processes. Add up the rows with the same name. Why it works: working set is the memory a process holds right now, shown here in MB. Ticket closed. (Resolution: RAM hog found.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Find the process that owns the port before you start killing things.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
$conn = Get-NetTCPConnection -LocalPort 8080 -State Listen
Get-Process -Id $conn.OwningProcess

Heads up: 8080 is an example. Use your port. Don't kill system processes. Why it works: the connection shows which process ID is listening. Ticket closed. (Resolution: port freed.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Don't trust the green icon. Check the service, real-time protection and when the definitions last updated.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Get-MpComputerStatus |
  Select-Object AMServiceEnabled, RealTimeProtectionEnabled,
  AntivirusSignatureLastUpdated

Heads up: Another antivirus can put Defender in passive mode. Check which one is in charge. Why it works: it reports the real state, not what the tray icon shows. Ticket closed. (Resolution: Defender checked.)

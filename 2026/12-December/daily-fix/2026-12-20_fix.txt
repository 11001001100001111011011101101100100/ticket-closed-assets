FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Event 41 means it restarted without a clean shutdown. Look at the last five.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Get-WinEvent -FilterHashtable @{ LogName = 'System'; Id = 41 } |
  Select-Object -First 5 TimeCreated, Id

Heads up: No results means no unexpected restarts were logged. Why it works: Kernel-Power 41 is Windows noting the last shutdown was not clean. Ticket closed. (Resolution: crash explained.)

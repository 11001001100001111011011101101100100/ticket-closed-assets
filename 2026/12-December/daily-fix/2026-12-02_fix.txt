FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Your temp folder is a junk drawer. Empty it. Anything in use is skipped, so it's safe.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Remove-Item "$env:TEMP\*" -Recurse -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue

Heads up: Close your apps first so fewer files are locked. Why it works: apps leave temp files behind and rarely clean up. Ticket closed. (Resolution: temp cleared.)

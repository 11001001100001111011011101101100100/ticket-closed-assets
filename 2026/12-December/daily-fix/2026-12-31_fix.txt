FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Before the year ends, check whether Windows is waiting for a restart.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
$k = "HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows"
Test-Path "$k\CurrentVersion\WindowsUpdate\Auto Update\RebootRequired"
Test-Path "$k\CurrentVersion\Component Based Servicing\RebootPending"

Heads up: True means a restart is pending. False doesn't rule out every case. Why it works: Windows sets these keys when an install needs a reboot to finish. Ticket closed. (Resolution: reboot checked.)

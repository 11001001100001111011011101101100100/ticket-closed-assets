FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Find who is locked, then unlock them. Check why before it happens again.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Search-ADAccount -LockedOut | Select-Object Name, SamAccountName
Unlock-ADAccount -Identity jsmith

Heads up: jsmith is an example. Old saved passwords on phones are a top cause. Why it works: a stale saved password somewhere keeps retrying and re-locks the account. Ticket closed. (Resolution: unlocked.)

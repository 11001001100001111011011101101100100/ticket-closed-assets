FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Slow PC? Rank the processes by how much processor time they've used. The top one is usually the answer.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-Process | Sort-Object CPU -Descending |
  Select-Object -First 10 Name, Id, CPU

Heads up: CPU here is total seconds used, not live percent. Run it twice and compare. Why it works: long-running heavy processes float to the top of the list. Ticket closed. (Resolution: culprit found.)

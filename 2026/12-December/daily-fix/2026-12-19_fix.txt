FACEBOOK CAPTION:
List the adapters, then restart the one that's misbehaving. Quicker than a full reboot.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Get-NetAdapter
Restart-NetAdapter -Name "Wi-Fi"

Heads up: Drops that connection briefly. Don't do it over a remote session on that adapter. Why it works: it resets the driver and link without a reboot. Ticket closed. (Resolution: adapter restarted.)

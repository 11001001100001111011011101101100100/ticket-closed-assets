FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Year-end audit. List the ten most recent updates and when each one landed.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-HotFix | Sort-Object InstalledOn -Descending |
  Select-Object -First 10 HotFixID, InstalledOn

Heads up: Not every update type shows up here. Check Update history for the full list. Why it works: it reads the installed update log, newest first. Ticket closed. (Resolution: patches listed.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Find the key before the prompt becomes a crisis. Check the protectors and the status.

Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run:
manage-bde -protectors -get C:
manage-bde -status C:

Heads up: The output shows a recovery password. Treat it like a password. Never post it. Why it works: the key is usually also stored in AD or your account. Ticket closed. (Resolution: key located.)

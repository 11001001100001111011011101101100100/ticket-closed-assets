FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Don't guess. Generate the battery report and compare design capacity to full charge capacity.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
powercfg /batteryreport /output "$env:USERPROFILE\battery.html"
Start-Process "$env:USERPROFILE\battery.html"

Heads up: Laptops only. A big gap between the two numbers means the battery is worn. Why it works: the report tracks health, cycles and recent usage. Ticket closed. (Resolution: battery diagnosed.)

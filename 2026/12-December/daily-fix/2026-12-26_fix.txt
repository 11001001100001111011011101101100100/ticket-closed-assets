FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Don't guess the version. Ask Windows for the edition, version and build number in one go.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-ComputerInfo |
  Select-Object WindowsProductName, WindowsVersion, OsBuildNumber

Heads up: Get-ComputerInfo can take ten seconds the first time you run it. Why it works: the build number is what update and support pages ask for. Ticket closed. (Resolution: version known.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Find the ten biggest files under Users before you delete anything.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Get-ChildItem C:\Users -Recurse -File -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue |
  Sort-Object Length -Descending |
  Select-Object -First 10 FullName, Length

Heads up: Slow on big drives. Don't delete anything you haven't checked. Why it works: Length is in bytes, sorted largest first. Ticket closed. (Resolution: space reclaimed.)

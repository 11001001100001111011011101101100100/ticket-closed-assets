FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Give people a five-minute warning and a message. Changed your mind? Abort it.

Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run:
shutdown /r /t 300 /m \\PC01 /c "IT restart in 5 minutes. Save your work."
# Changed your mind?
shutdown /a /m \\PC01

Heads up: PC01 is an example. 300 is seconds. Why it works: the timer gives users a warning, and /a cancels it. Ticket closed. (Resolution: restart announced.)

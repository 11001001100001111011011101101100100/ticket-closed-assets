TITLE: SUPPORT WANTS THE SERIAL NUMBER?
HOST: David (IT)
IMAGE: 2026-12-10_fix.jpg (1080x1350)
TESTED: command logic reviewed, not run on a live endpoint.

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
No flipping the laptop over. Ask Windows for the serial number, make and model.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Get-CimInstance Win32_BIOS | Select-Object SerialNumber
Get-CimInstance Win32_ComputerSystem |
  Select-Object Manufacturer, Model

Heads up: Some custom builds return a blank or placeholder serial. Why it works: the BIOS stores it, so Windows can read it without a screwdriver. Ticket closed. (Resolution: serial found.)

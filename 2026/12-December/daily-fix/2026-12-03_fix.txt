FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Wrong time breaks sign-in and HTTPS. Check the offset, then force a sync.

Open Command Prompt as Administrator and run:
w32tm /query /status
w32tm /resync

Heads up: Domain PCs sync from the domain. A bad offset can mean a deeper problem. Why it works: Kerberos and certificates need clocks within minutes of each other. Ticket closed. (Resolution: clock synced.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Skip the settings maze. One command reports the activation status and expiry.

Open Command Prompt and run:
cscript //nologo %windir%\system32\slmgr.vbs /xpr

Heads up: Volume-licensed PCs may show an expiry date. That is normal for KMS. Why it works: slmgr reads the license state straight from Windows. Ticket closed. (Resolution: activation checked.)

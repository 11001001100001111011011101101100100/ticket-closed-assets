FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Happy Data Privacy Day. Locking takes a second and keeps your session yours.

Open Command Prompt and run:
rundll32.exe user32.dll,LockWorkStation
# Or press Win + L

Heads up: Win + L is faster. Use the command in a script or shortcut. Why it works: an unlocked PC is open to anyone who walks past. Ticket closed. (Resolution: PC locked.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Show the extensions. invoice.pdf.exe looks very different when you can read the .exe.

Open PowerShell and run:
$p = "HKCU:\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Explorer\Advanced"
Set-ItemProperty -Path $p -Name HideFileExt -Value 0
Stop-Process -Name explorer -Force

Heads up: Explorer restarts and open folder windows close. Save your work first. Why it works: Windows hides known extensions by default, and attackers count on it. Ticket closed. (Resolution: extensions visible.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Calendar invites off by an hour? Check the time zone, then set the right one.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Get-TimeZone
Set-TimeZone -Id "Eastern Standard Time"

Heads up: Eastern is an example. See every ID with Get-TimeZone -ListAvailable. Why it works: meetings, logs and Kerberos all depend on the right zone. Ticket closed. (Resolution: zone fixed.)

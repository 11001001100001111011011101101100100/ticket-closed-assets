FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Zip the folder from the command line. One file instead of fifty attachments.

Open PowerShell and run:
Compress-Archive -Path "$env:USERPROFILE\Documents\Project" `
  -DestinationPath "$env:USERPROFILE\Desktop\Project.zip"

Heads up: Example paths. Compress-Archive can fail on files over 2 GB in Windows PowerShell 5.1. Why it works: it packs the whole folder into one compressed file. Ticket closed. (Resolution: zipped.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Ask the remote PC when it last started. No need to bother the user.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
(Get-CimInstance Win32_OperatingSystem `
  -ComputerName PC01).LastBootUpTime

Heads up: PC01 is an example. Needs remote management access and a free firewall path. Why it works: the OS records its last boot time, and CIM can read it remotely. Ticket closed. (Resolution: uptime known.)

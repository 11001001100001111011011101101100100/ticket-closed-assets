FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Before you ask for access, see which groups your account is actually in.

Open Command Prompt and run:
whoami /groups

Heads up: New group memberships only show after you sign out and back in. Why it works: access is decided by the groups in your sign-in token. Ticket closed. (Resolution: groups checked.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Know who has the keys. List the members of the local Administrators group.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Get-LocalGroupMember -Group Administrators

Heads up: Unexpected names deserve a closer look. Don't remove anyone until you know why they're there. Why it works: admin sprawl is how small mistakes become big ones. Ticket closed. (Resolution: admins reviewed.)

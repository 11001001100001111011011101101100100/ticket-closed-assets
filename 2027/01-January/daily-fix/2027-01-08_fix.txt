FACEBOOK CAPTION:
A forgotten proxy setting can break browsing and updates. Check both places.

Open PowerShell and run:
netsh winhttp show proxy
$p = "HKCU:\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion"
Get-ItemProperty "$p\Internet Settings" |
  Select-Object ProxyEnable, ProxyServer

Heads up: ProxyEnable 1 means the user proxy is on. A work PC may need one on purpose. Why it works: WinHTTP and the user settings use different proxy values. Ticket closed. (Resolution: proxy found.)

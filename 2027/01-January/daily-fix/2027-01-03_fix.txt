FACEBOOK CAPTION:
List the files in Documents that changed in the last day. The logs and the file system agree more often than people do.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-ChildItem "$env:USERPROFILE\Documents" -Recurse -File |
  Where-Object LastWriteTime -gt (Get-Date).AddDays(-1) |
  Select-Object FullName, LastWriteTime

Heads up: Slow on big folders. Change the path to check another location. Why it works: every file records when it was last written. Ticket closed. (Resolution: changes found.)

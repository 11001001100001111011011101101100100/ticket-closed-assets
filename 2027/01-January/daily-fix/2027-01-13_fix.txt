FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Pull the last ten system errors from the past hour. That is usually where the story starts.

Open PowerShell and run:
$f = @{ LogName='System'; Level=2;
       StartTime=(Get-Date).AddHours(-1) }
Get-WinEvent -FilterHashtable $f -MaxEvents 10

Heads up: No errors in the window? PowerShell says no events were found. That's good news. Why it works: Level 2 means Error, and the filter keeps only the last hour. Ticket closed. (Resolution: errors found.)

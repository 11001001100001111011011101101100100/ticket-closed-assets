FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Allow-listing a device? Get the adapter name, status and MAC address.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-NetAdapter | Select-Object Name, Status, MacAddress, LinkSpeed

Heads up: Wi-Fi may use a random address per network. That's a privacy feature. Why it works: each adapter reports its physical address and link speed. Ticket closed. (Resolution: MAC found.)

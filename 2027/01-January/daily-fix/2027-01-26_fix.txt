FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Trace the route. The hop where it stops tells you which side to blame.

Open PowerShell and run:
Test-NetConnection 8.8.8.8 -TraceRoute

Heads up: A timeout partway doesn't always mean a problem. Look at where it truly ends. Why it works: it lists each hop between your PC and the target. Ticket closed. (Resolution: path traced.)

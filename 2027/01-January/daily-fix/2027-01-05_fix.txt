FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Wrong printer, ghost printer, three of the same printer? List what's actually installed.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-Printer | Select-Object Name, DriverName, PortName

Heads up: To remove one, use Remove-Printer with the exact name. Check the list first. Why it works: you see the driver and port behind each printer. Ticket closed. (Resolution: printers listed.)

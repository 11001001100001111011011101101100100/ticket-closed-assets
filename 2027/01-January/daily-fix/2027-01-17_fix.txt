FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Hard-coded DNS from an old fix? Put the adapter back to getting DNS automatically.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Set-DnsClientServerAddress -InterfaceAlias "Wi-Fi" -ResetServerAddresses

Heads up: Don't use this on a PC that needs static DNS. Check the adapter name first. Why it works: it clears the manual servers so DHCP supplies them again. Ticket closed. (Resolution: DNS reset.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Sort the installed drivers by date and look at the oldest ones first.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-CimInstance Win32_PnPSignedDriver |
  Where-Object DeviceName | Sort-Object DriverDate |
  Select-Object -First 10 DeviceName, DriverVersion, DriverDate

Heads up: Old doesn't mean broken. Update from the maker's site, not a driver tool. Why it works: each driver records its version and release date. Ticket closed. (Resolution: drivers listed.)

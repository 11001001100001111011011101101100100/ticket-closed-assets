FACEBOOK CAPTION:
List the services set to Automatic that aren't running. Not every one is a problem, but this is the shortlist.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-Service |
  Where-Object { $_.StartType -eq 'Automatic' -and $_.Status -ne 'Running' } |
  Select-Object Name, Status

Heads up: Some automatic services stop on purpose after finishing their job. Why it works: it filters to services that should be running and aren't. Ticket closed. (Resolution: services found.)

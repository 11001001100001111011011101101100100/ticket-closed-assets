FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Two things block most upgrades. Check Secure Boot and the TPM.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Confirm-SecureBootUEFI
Get-Tpm

Heads up: Confirm-SecureBootUEFI errors on older BIOS-mode PCs. That result is useful too. Why it works: Windows 11 needs both Secure Boot capability and a TPM. Ticket closed. (Resolution: Windows 11 ready.)

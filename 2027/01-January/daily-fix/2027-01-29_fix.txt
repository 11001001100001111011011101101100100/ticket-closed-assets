FACEBOOK CAPTION:
OS, version, boot time and memory in one command, filtered so you don't scroll.

Open Command Prompt and run:
systeminfo | findstr /B /C:"OS Name" /C:"OS Version"
  /C:"System Boot Time" /C:"Total Physical Memory"

Heads up: systeminfo takes a few seconds to run. Output follows your system language. Why it works: findstr keeps just the lines you asked for. Ticket closed. (Resolution: specs listed.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Get the address, gateway and DNS in one view. Watch for anything starting with 169.254.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-NetIPConfiguration

Heads up: An address starting 169.254 means DHCP failed. Check the cable, Wi-Fi or DHCP server. Why it works: it shows the IP, gateway and DNS for every active adapter. Ticket closed. (Resolution: IP found.)

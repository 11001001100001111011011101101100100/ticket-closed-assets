FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Before you re-pair anything, restart the Bluetooth service.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Restart-Service -Name bthserv
Get-Service -Name bthserv

Heads up: Connected devices may drop for a few seconds while it restarts. Why it works: the service can stall after sleep or a driver change. Ticket closed. (Resolution: Bluetooth restarted.)

FACEBOOK CAPTION:
List the power plans, then switch to Balanced.

Open Command Prompt and run:
powercfg /list
powercfg /setactive SCHEME_BALANCED

Heads up: SCHEME_BALANCED is the built-in Balanced plan. Check the list first. Why it works: the power plan caps how hard the CPU is allowed to work. Ticket closed. (Resolution: plan set.)

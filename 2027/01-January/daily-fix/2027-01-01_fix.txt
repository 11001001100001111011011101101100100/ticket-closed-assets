FACEBOOK CAPTION:
New year, check the disk before it checks you. Free and used space per drive.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-PSDrive -PSProvider FileSystem |
  Select-Object Name,
  @{n='UsedGB'; e={[math]::Round($_.Used/1GB,1)}},
  @{n='FreeGB'; e={[math]::Round($_.Free/1GB,1)}}

Heads up: Network drives can show blank values. Look at the local ones. Why it works: it reads the drive's used and free bytes and converts them to GB. Ticket closed. (Resolution: space known.)

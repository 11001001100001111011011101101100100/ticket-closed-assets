FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Month end. Search for it by extension instead of by memory.

Open PowerShell and run:
Get-ChildItem C:\Users -Recurse -Filter "*.xlsx" `
  -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue |
  Select-Object -First 20 FullName, LastWriteTime

Heads up: Slow on big drives. Change *.xlsx to the type you need. Why it works: the filter matches the extension while it walks the folders. Ticket closed. (Resolution: file found.)

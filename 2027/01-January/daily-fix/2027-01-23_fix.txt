FACEBOOK CAPTION:
Ask Windows what it thinks of the drive. Health and operational status for each disk.

Open PowerShell as Administrator and run:
Get-PhysicalDisk |
  Select-Object FriendlyName, MediaType, HealthStatus, OperationalStatus

Heads up: Healthy here doesn't promise the drive is fine. Keep backups. Why it works: Windows tracks drive health through the storage stack. Ticket closed. (Resolution: disk healthy.)
